5 Best Free SMS Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated November 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Your phone already has a texting app — so why install another one? Because the stock app on many phones is slow, ugly, or missing the basics: scheduled messages, spam blocking, proper search and a dark theme that actually works. We tested the most popular free SMS apps on a mid-range Android phone for one question: which one sends a plain text message best, without ads or a subscription getting in the way?

What we tested

  • Everyday texting — sending, receiving, group chats and MMS photos
  • Spam blocking — how well each app keeps junk texts out of your inbox
  • Useful extras — scheduled messages, search, backup, dual-SIM support
  • Ads & cost — what is genuinely free and what quietly asks for money

1. Google Messages — best for most people

Google Messages is the default on most Android phones for a reason: it is fast, clean and free, with RCS chat (typing indicators, read receipts and high-quality photos) where your carrier supports it, plus built-in spam protection that quietly filters junk. If you just want texting to work, start here — you may already have it installed.

2. Textra SMS — best customisation

Textra is the classic replacement texting app: hundreds of bubble styles and colours, per-contact notifications, scheduled messages and a quick-reply popup that works from the lock screen. All features are free; you will occasionally see an ad, or you can make a one-time purchase to remove ads forever. If you like your phone to look exactly how you want it, this is the one.

3. QKSMS — best open-source classic

QKSMS is completely free, ad-free and open source, with a clean design, scheduled messages and per-conversation theming. One honest warning: it has not received a major update since 2021, so it lacks newer features like RCS. It still works well as a plain, private SMS app — just know it is no longer actively developed.

4. Chomp SMS — best for power features

Chomp packs in everything Textra does plus a passcode lock for private conversations, SMS backup, signatures, text snippets and strong dual-SIM and group-messaging support. Like Textra, all features are free with occasional ads, removable with a one-time purchase. Best if you send a lot of texts and want every tool available.

5. Fossify Messages — best ad-free open-source app

Fossify Messages is the modern, actively maintained successor to the old Simple Mobile Tools SMS app: fully free, no ads, no trackers, with scheduled messages, search and a simple clean interface. It does not do RCS, but for plain SMS it is the most honest option on this list — nothing to buy, nothing watching you.

Our verdict: install Google Messages first — it is already on most phones, it is free, and its spam filtering alone is worth it. Only switch to Textra if you want deep customisation, or to Fossify Messages if you want a fully ad-free, open-source app with nothing to sell you. Avoid "free" SMS apps that ask for your contacts and message history in exchange for flashy themes.
