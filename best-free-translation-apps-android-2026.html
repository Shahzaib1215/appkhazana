5 Best Free Translation Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated November 14, 2026 · 7 min read

A good translator app is the difference between reading a menu and guessing at it. We tested the most popular free translation apps on a mid-range Android phone for one question: which one translates accurately, works offline, and doesn't lock the useful features behind a subscription?

What we tested

  • Offline translation — downloadable language packs that work with no internet
  • Camera & conversation modes — translating signs, menus and live speech
  • Language coverage — how many languages, and how good the quality is for the common ones
  • Free-tier honesty — what you actually get free vs. what needs a paid plan

1. Google Translate — best overall, best offline

Google Translate covers the most languages, its camera mode translates signs and menus live on screen, and its conversation mode handles two-way talk well. Offline language packs are free and genuinely usable — download your languages before a trip and the app keeps working on a plane. It is free, with no subscription at all.

2. Microsoft Translator — best for conversations

Microsoft Translator's multi-person conversation mode lets several people join a chat in their own languages — genuinely useful for group travel or meetings. It also offers offline packs, camera translation and a phrasebook. Free, with no paywall on the core features.

3. DeepL — best translation quality

DeepL consistently produces the most natural-sounding translations for the major European languages, and its writing-style alternatives help you pick the right tone. The trade-off is coverage: far fewer languages than Google or Microsoft, and no camera mode. Best if you mostly translate between English and European languages.

4. SayHi Translate — best for spoken translation

SayHi is built around voice: tap, speak, and hear the translation spoken back. Its conversation mode is simple and fast, which makes it a good pick for quick spoken exchanges abroad. The free tier covers the basics; some voices and features are paid.

5. Papago — best for Asian languages

Naver's Papago shines for Korean, Japanese and Chinese — languages where other apps often stumble. It offers text, voice, image and conversation translation, plus a dictionary and phrasebook. Free, and the best companion app if you are travelling in East Asia.

Our verdict: install Google Translate first — it is free, covers the most languages, and its offline packs mean it works anywhere. Add Papago if you travel in East Asia, or DeepL if you translate a lot of European-language text and want the most natural wording. Skip any "translator" app that demands a subscription before it translates a single word.
