Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

AppKhazana ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Analytics: we may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which articles readers like. This data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Contact messages: if you email us, we keep your message only to respond — we never sell or share it.

What we never do

  • We do not require accounts or collect personal details to read articles.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not distribute software — all download links go to official app stores.

External links

Our articles link to the Google Play Store and other official sites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
