5 Best Free Wallpaper Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated November 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Your home screen is the first thing you see every time you unlock your phone — and the stock wallpaper gets boring fast. We tested the most popular free wallpaper apps on a mid-range Android phone for one question: which one gives you genuinely good-looking wallpapers without ads, watermarks, or a paywall on every second image?

What we tested

  • Image quality — sharp on a modern phone screen, no blurry upscaled photos
  • Ads & paywalls — whether the good wallpapers are actually free or locked behind a subscription
  • Fresh content — new wallpapers added regularly, not the same ten images forever
  • Everyday use — setting home and lock screen separately, daily auto-change, categories that make sense

1. Google Wallpapers — best free, no-nonsense option

Google's own wallpaper app is the cleanest starting point: curated collections (Google Earth, landscapes, art, cityscapes), a daily wallpaper that changes automatically, and zero ads. It is free, with no subscription and no locked collections. The library is smaller than the big wallpaper stores, but everything in it is high quality and properly sized for your screen.

2. Zedge — biggest library, but ads included

Zedge has the largest wallpaper and ringtone library of any app here, with categories for almost everything and a huge community uploading new images daily. The trade-off is real: the free version shows ads, and some premium wallpapers need Zedge credits or a subscription. If you want the widest choice and don't mind ads, this is where you'll find it.

3. Backdrops — best curated artist wallpapers

Backdrops is built around wallpapers made by real artists, with new ones added daily and a community section where anyone can submit designs. The free version shows ads, and a paid Pro upgrade unlocks the full collection — but plenty of genuinely beautiful wallpapers are free. If you want wallpapers that look designed rather than downloaded, start here.

4. Muzei Live Wallpaper — best live wallpaper, free forever

Muzei turns your home screen into a slowly changing art gallery: it rotates a famous artwork every day, gently blurred so your icons stay readable, and it can also rotate your own photos. It is free, with no ads, and you can add sources for photography, space images and more. The catch is that it is a live wallpaper, so it uses a little more battery than a static image.

5. Walli — best for supporting artists

Walli features wallpapers made by independent artists, and artists earn money when their work is downloaded — so your home screen actually supports the people who made it. The free version includes ads, and some wallpapers are premium, but the free collection is large and consistently good-looking, with new artist packs added regularly.

Our verdict: install Google Wallpapers first — it is free forever, has no ads, and its daily wallpaper keeps your home screen fresh with zero effort. If you want more variety, add Backdrops for artist designs or Muzei for a rotating art gallery. Only pick Zedge if you specifically want the biggest library and can tolerate ads in the free version.
