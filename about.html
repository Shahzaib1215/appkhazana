About AppKhazana

Who we are and how we test.

AppKhazana ("treasure of apps") started with a simple frustration: every "top 10 apps" list on the internet is either sponsored or copied from another site. We wanted reviews written by someone who actually installed the app and used it.

How we test

  1. Real devices: every app is installed on a real mid-range Android phone — not an emulator, not a spec sheet.
  2. Real tasks: we give every app the same job (edit the same video, enhance the same photo) and compare results.
  3. Free tier first: we test what you get without paying, because that is what most readers will use.
  4. Official downloads only: every download button on this site goes to the Google Play Store. We do not link to cracked, modded, or "premium unlocked" APKs — those are illegal and often carry malware.

How we earn

This site is supported by display ads and, in the future, affiliate links to legitimate software. Our reviews are never paid for — a sponsor cannot buy a better ranking here.
