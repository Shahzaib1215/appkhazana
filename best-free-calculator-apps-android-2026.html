5 Best Free Calculator Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated November 23, 2026 · 7 min read

Every Android phone ships with a calculator — so why install another one? Because the stock app is usually bare-bones: no history, no unit converter, no scientific mode worth using. We tested the most popular free calculator apps on a mid-range Android phone for one question: which one does the maths you actually need, without ads popping up mid-calculation?

What we tested

  • Everyday maths — fast, accurate basic arithmetic with a readable history
  • Scientific & graphing — trig, logs, fractions and plotted graphs for students
  • Extras that matter — unit and currency converters, floating window, widgets
  • Ads & privacy — a calculator has no business tracking you or showing video ads

1. Google Calculator — best for most people

Clean, fast, completely free and ad-free. It handles basic and scientific maths, keeps a history of your calculations, and matches your phone's theme. If your phone didn't ship with it, install this first — for everyday sums, it is all you need.

2. Calculator++ — best all-rounder

Calculator++ adds what Google's misses: a built-in unit converter, currency converter, floating window that hovers over other apps, and home-screen widgets. The free version is supported by ads, which is the only real downside.

3. HiPER Scientific Calculator — best for students and engineers

HiPER is a serious scientific calculator: fractions, complex numbers, matrices, equation solving and a huge function library, all in a layout that fits a phone screen. The free version shows ads; a one-time payment removes them.

4. Desmos Graphing Calculator — best free graphing calculator

Desmos plots functions beautifully, handles tables and sliders, and costs nothing with no ads at all. If you are a student who needs graphs rather than just answers, this is the one to install — it is the same engine teachers use in classrooms.

5. ClevCalc — best for quick conversions

ClevCalc bundles a calculator with a currency converter, unit converter and a floating mini-calculator you can open over any app — handy when you are shopping online and need a quick price conversion. The free version includes ads.
